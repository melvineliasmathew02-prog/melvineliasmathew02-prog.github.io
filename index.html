// ABOUT

Robotics, from the R&D bench to the field

I'm Melvin — a robotics engineer from Kerala, India, with a B.Tech in Robotics and Automation. For 23 months I was the sole engineer owning ROS development in an R&D team building autonomous mobile robots (AMRs) for manufacturing — everything from URDF modelling and control algorithms to sensor calibration and field diagnostics. That work landed on ±5mm localization accuracy and stable autonomous navigation both indoors and outdoors.

Right now I'm working on the other side of autonomy: collecting and structuring the data that trains humanoid robots to handle everyday tasks like cleaning and sorting. I'm looking for robotics engineer, robotics deployment engineer, or ROS developer roles — open to relocating internationally, Ireland especially.
